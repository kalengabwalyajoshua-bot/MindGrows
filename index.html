How It Works

🛡️ Important Disclaimer

This is a simulation tool, not medical or professional advice.

The Human Digital Twin Future Simulator uses rule-based algorithms to project potential outcomes based on your daily habits. It is designed for educational and self-reflection purposes only.

⚠️ Do not use this tool as a substitute for professional medical, psychological, or academic counseling.

🔬 How Scores Are Calculated

📈 Productivity Score

Formula:

Base = (Study Hours × 10)
Sleep Bonus = (Sleep Quality × 10)
Screen Penalty = -(Screen Time × 3)
Stress Penalty = -(Stress Level × 2)
Exercise Bonus = (Exercise Days × 3)

Final = Base + Sleep Bonus + Screen Penalty + Stress Penalty + Exercise Bonus

Normalized to 0-100 scale. Higher study hours and better sleep increase productivity.

⚠️ Burnout Risk

Formula:

Risk = (Stress Level × 10) + (Screen Time × 2)
Sleep Factor = -(Sleep Hours × 5)
Exercise Factor = -(Exercise Days × 3)

Final = Risk + Sleep Factor + Exercise Factor

Normalized to 0-100 scale. High stress and screen time increase burnout risk. Better sleep and exercise reduce it.

🎓 Academic Trajectory

Formula:

Base = (Study Hours × 8)
Focus Quality = (8 - Screen Time) × 5
Cognitive Health = (Sleep Hours × 6) - (Stress Level × 3)
Consistency = (Exercise Days × 2)

Final = Base + Focus Quality + Cognitive Health + Consistency

Normalized to 0-100 scale. Balanced habits lead to better academic outcomes.

🧠 Mental Well-being

Formula:

Base = 50
Sleep Quality = (Sleep Hours × 5)
Stress Impact = -(Stress Level × 5)
Exercise Benefit = (Exercise Days × 4)
Screen Impact = -(Screen Time × 2)

Final = Base + Sleep Quality + Stress Impact + Exercise Benefit + Screen Impact

Normalized to 0-100 scale. Sleep, exercise, and low stress improve mental health.
